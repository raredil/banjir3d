# ADR 0002 — DEMNAS kept private until its licence is known
Date: 2026-10-23 · Status: accepted

DEMNAS terms of redistribution could not be found online (portal blocked from the build environment, no statement in search results). The owner uploads tiles to a **draft** release `demnas-private` (visible only to repo collaborators). `demnas.yml` builds an 8 m UTM mosaic back into the same draft. Nothing derived from DEMNAS goes on the public site or in public releases until the licence is read (owner sends a screenshot of the portal terms). Public terrain meanwhile: Copernicus GLO-30 (DSM) and FABDEM V1-2 (bare earth).
